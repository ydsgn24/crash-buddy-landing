These Shop Services Terms (“Services Terms”) govern the remote collision estimating services provided by Crash Buddy LLC, doing business as Crash Buddy Estimating (“Crash Buddy Estimating,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) to automotive repair businesses and other authorized business customers (“Shop,” “you,” or “your”).

These Services Terms apply to Estimates, Supplements, revisions, Special Orders, Claims Hub, and related estimating services (collectively, the “Services”).

By affirmatively accepting these Services Terms when placing an order, or through an applicable authorized onboarding process, the Shop agrees to the terms presented at that time.

A separately negotiated written agreement signed by both parties controls over these Services Terms to the extent of any conflict.

1. Eligibility and Authority

The Services are intended for legitimate automotive repair businesses and other authorized commercial customers.

The Shop represents that:

  • It has authority to request and purchase the Services.
  • The person accepting these Services Terms is authorized to act on its behalf.
  • It has the necessary rights and permissions to provide submitted information.
  • It will use the Services for lawful business purposes.
  • It will comply with applicable repair, consumer-protection, privacy, and insurance-related laws.

The Shop is responsible for its authorized users and the information they submit.

2. Description of Services

Crash Buddy Estimating provides remote collision estimating support.

At the Shop's request, we may prepare:

  • Initial Estimates.
  • Supplements.
  • Revisions to existing estimating documents.
  • Special Orders requiring additional research.
  • Related estimating documentation.

Our work is based on the photographs, documents, vehicle information, instructions, and other materials provided by the Shop, together with relevant estimating resources available to our team.

We may prepare estimating documents using the Shop's business information when authorized.

Our normal workflow is:

The Shop sends the information. We prepare the document. The Shop reviews it and decides how to use it.

The Services do not include physical vehicle inspections, repair work, insurance coverage determinations, or claim settlement services.

3. Remote Estimating Limitations

Crash Buddy Estimating generally prepares documents without physically inspecting the vehicle.

Our estimators may rely on photographs, teardown documentation, vehicle identification information, existing estimates, Shop instructions, and available third-party estimating resources.

Photographs and documents may not reveal every damaged component, hidden condition, mechanical issue, structural concern, or repair requirement.

An Estimate or Supplement reflects the information reasonably available at the time of preparation.

Additional damage, changes in repair procedures, parts availability, pricing, or other information may require revisions or additional estimating work.

The Shop acknowledges these limitations when requesting remote estimating services.

4. Mandatory Shop Review and Approval

Every Estimate, Supplement, revision, and related document must be reviewed and approved by the Shop before the Shop relies on it for repairs, provides it to a customer, or submits it to an insurer or another third party.

The Shop is responsible for verifying, as applicable:

  • Vehicle identity and configuration.
  • Documented damage and repair operations.
  • Repair-versus-replacement decisions.
  • Labor operations, rates, and times.
  • Parts selection and pricing.
  • Applicable OEM procedures.
  • Customer authorizations.
  • Repair methodology and safety requirements.
  • The accuracy and completeness of the document.

A document marked as completed or available for download is delivered for the Shop's review. Its availability does not constitute the Shop's approval.

The Shop must notify Crash Buddy Estimating of suspected errors or omissions and request appropriate corrections before relying on the affected information.

The Shop's review obligation does not eliminate any legal responsibility Crash Buddy Estimating may have for its own services.

5. Repair Decisions and Shop Responsibilities

The Shop retains responsibility for the physical vehicle and all repair-related decisions.

This includes:

  • Inspecting and diagnosing the vehicle.
  • Identifying hidden or additional damage.
  • Performing teardown and measurements.
  • Determining safe and appropriate repair procedures.
  • Verifying current OEM repair requirements.
  • Selecting and installing parts.
  • Obtaining required customer authorizations.
  • Complying with applicable repair-facility laws.
  • Performing and documenting repairs.
  • Preparing final invoices.
  • Communicating with customers and insurers.
  • Deciding whether and how estimating documents are used or submitted.

Crash Buddy Estimating does not perform vehicle repairs or independently certify that a repair plan is safe or complete.

Our estimating documents are not a substitute for the Shop's professional judgment, physical inspection, or legal obligations.

6. Insurance Matters and No Claim Settlement Authority

Crash Buddy Estimating prepares estimating documentation for repair businesses.

Unless a separate legally reviewed written agreement expressly provides otherwise, we do not:

  • Act as an insurance company.
  • Determine insurance coverage.
  • Interpret or decide policy benefits.
  • Determine legal liability for a loss.
  • Settle insurance claims.
  • Negotiate claim settlements on behalf of the Shop or vehicle owner.
  • Bind an insurer to a payment decision.
  • Submit insurance claims on behalf of the Shop.
  • Act as a public insurance adjuster.
  • Authorize repairs on behalf of a vehicle owner.

The Shop controls its insurer communications, document submissions, repair authorizations, and claim-related decisions.

Nothing in these Services Terms authorizes Crash Buddy Estimating to perform activities requiring a professional or regulatory license that it does not hold.

7. No Guarantee of Insurance Approval or Payment

Crash Buddy Estimating does not guarantee that an insurer or another third party will:

  • Accept an Estimate or Supplement.
  • Approve any particular operation or part.
  • Agree with a labor rate or repair methodology.
  • Reimburse a particular amount.
  • Pay a claim.
  • Reach a particular settlement outcome.

Insurer decisions and reimbursement amounts are outside our control.

The Shop remains responsible for determining whether and how to submit or rely on the estimating documentation.

The Shop is responsible for providing reasonably complete and accurate information necessary for the requested work.

This may include:

  • VIN, year, make, model, trim, and options.
  • Vehicle damage photographs.
  • Teardown and additional-damage photographs.
  • Existing insurance estimates.
  • Claim and insurer reference information.
  • Shop labor rates and repair preferences.
  • Parts information.
  • Repair-plan instructions.
  • Relevant customer or claim documents.

Crash Buddy Estimating may reasonably rely on information provided by the Shop.

Incomplete, inaccurate, unclear, or outdated information may affect the resulting document and require additional work.

We may request clarification or additional documentation before continuing an order.

12. Estimates and Supplements

An Estimate generally involves preparation of an initial estimating document based on the submitted vehicle and damage information.

A Supplement generally involves additional estimating documentation for damage, operations, or information associated with an existing repair estimate or claim.

The precise work required depends on the submitted materials and the scope accepted for the order.

A Supplement is not a guarantee that additional amounts will be paid by an insurer.

13. Revisions and Additional Work

The Shop may request corrections or changes to a delivered Estimate or Supplement.

A revision that remains within the original scope, such as correcting an identified operation or updating information related to the same documented damage, does not automatically constitute a new paid Supplement.

Requests involving newly discovered damage, additional repair phases, materially expanded operations, or substantially different information may require a new paid order.

Crash Buddy Estimating will reasonably determine whether a request falls within the original scope or requires additional paid work.

If an additional charge is required, the Shop will be informed of the applicable price before authorizing that additional work.

We will not automatically charge a new Supplement fee merely because the Shop reports a potential error in our original work.

Requests for correction should be made promptly and include sufficient supporting information for review.

14. Special Orders

A Special Order is estimating work requiring substantially more research or manual effort than a standard Estimate or Supplement.

Examples may include:

  • Vehicles not adequately supported by available estimating databases.
  • Classic, specialty, or uncommon vehicles.
  • Extensive OEM research.
  • Unusual parts research.
  • Dealer or manufacturer information requests.
  • Other materially research-intensive work.

A vehicle does not qualify as a Special Order solely because it is expensive or a luxury model.

Special Orders may have individually quoted prices.

The Shop must receive and accept the applicable custom price before the Special Order is authorized and charged.

15. Pricing

Our current standard public pricing is:

Estimate — US$105 per order

Supplement — US$145 per order

These are per-job service charges, not monthly subscription fees.

There are no standard monthly subscription fees or minimum order requirements for one-time orders.

Some Shops may have separately negotiated pricing.

Custom pricing, where applicable, will be communicated through Claims Hub, checkout, or a written pricing agreement.

Special Orders may be priced separately.

The price presented and accepted for a particular order governs that order, subject to any clearly disclosed applicable taxes or additional charges.

Prices may be changed prospectively. Changes do not retroactively increase the accepted price of an existing paid order.

Claims Hub is a business workflow tool.
