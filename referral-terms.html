These Referral Program Terms (“Referral Terms”) govern the body shop referral program (“Program”) operated by Crash Buddy LLC, doing business as Crash Buddy Estimating (“Crash Buddy Estimating,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

The Program allows eligible individuals or businesses (“Referrers”) to introduce new automotive repair businesses (“Referred Shops”) to Crash Buddy Estimating and potentially earn a referral reward.

By participating in the Program and affirmatively accepting these Referral Terms where acceptance is requested, you agree to the applicable terms.

Participation is also subject to our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

1. The Referral Offer

An eligible Referrer may earn a US$250 reward when a qualifying Referred Shop completes its first three qualifying paid Supplements with Crash Buddy Estimating.

The standard reward is:

US$250 per qualifying Referred Shop.

Only one standard referral reward is available for each qualifying Referred Shop.

Additional Estimates, Supplements, or future purchases by the same Shop do not generate additional standard referral rewards.

The Program is not a recurring commission arrangement.

2. Qualifying Supplements

A qualifying Supplement must:

  • Be an actual Supplement order placed by the Referred Shop.
  • Be successfully paid.
  • Be completed by Crash Buddy Estimating.
  • Remain valid and not be cancelled, refunded, reversed, duplicated, or determined to be fraudulent.

Estimate orders do not count toward the three-Supplement requirement.

Unpaid orders, cancelled orders, duplicate transactions, and invalid or fraudulent orders do not qualify.

If a previously counted transaction is refunded or reversed before the reward is approved, it will no longer count toward eligibility.

Three qualifying Supplements must be associated with the same eligible Referred Shop.

3. Eligible Referrers

Participation is open to individuals and businesses legally permitted to receive referral compensation.

Referrers must provide accurate identifying and payment information when reasonably requested.

Referrers are responsible for confirming that participation does not violate applicable laws, employment obligations, professional restrictions, or other binding agreements.

We may decline participation where payment would be unlawful or would create a prohibited conflict of interest.

Employees, contractors, and other persons with a business relationship with Crash Buddy Estimating may be subject to additional written referral or compensation rules.

4. Eligible Referred Shops

A Referred Shop must be a legitimate automotive repair business.

For the standard new-Shop referral reward, the Shop must not already be an established Crash Buddy Estimating customer before the qualifying referral is recorded.

A referral does not qualify merely because an existing customer submits additional orders.

The referred business must independently choose whether to use Crash Buddy Estimating.

No Referrer may create fictitious accounts, submit orders on behalf of a Shop without authorization, or manipulate transactions to obtain a reward.

5. Referral Submission and Attribution

Referrals should be submitted through an official Crash Buddy Estimating referral form, authorized referral link, or another method expressly accepted by us.

A referral must contain sufficient accurate information to identify the Referred Shop and establish attribution.

We may use submission records, referral identifiers, order history, and other reasonably reliable information to verify eligibility.

Submitting a referral does not guarantee exclusive rights to that Shop.

If multiple Referrers claim the same Shop, only one standard reward may be approved.

Attribution will be determined using available records and reasonable, consistently applied Program rules.

We may request additional information to resolve duplicate or disputed referrals.

We will not knowingly approve multiple standard rewards for the same qualifying Shop.

6. Self-Referrals and Prohibited Activity

The following are not eligible:

  • Referring your own Shop or a business you control for the purpose of receiving a reward.
  • Creating fictitious or duplicate Shops.
  • Submitting false referral information.
  • Manipulating transactions, refunds, or orders.
  • Impersonating Crash Buddy Estimating or another business.
  • Making misleading claims about our Services.
  • Using unauthorized access to customer information.
  • Engaging in unlawful spam or deceptive marketing.
  • Offering improper payments or benefits to employees or representatives of a Referred Shop.
  • Otherwise attempting to obtain rewards through fraud or abuse.

We may investigate suspected abuse and reject invalid referrals based on documented, reasonable grounds.

7. Referral Review and Reward Approval

A referral becomes eligible for reward review after the Referred Shop completes three qualifying paid Supplements.

Crash Buddy Estimating will review whether the referral satisfies the applicable Program requirements.

The reward is approved when eligibility has been verified.

Completion of three orders does not itself mean a payment has already been issued.

We may request information reasonably necessary to verify the Referrer, confirm eligibility, or satisfy legal and tax obligations.

Approval will not be unreasonably withheld for a referral that satisfies all applicable Program requirements.

We may reject a referral for fraud, ineligibility, duplicate attribution, unlawful conduct, or another documented violation of these Referral Terms.

8. Reward Payment

Approved referral rewards will be paid using a payment method designated or agreed upon by Crash Buddy Estimating.

The Referrer may be required to provide accurate payment details and any legally required tax information before payment can be processed.

Payment may be delayed where information is missing, eligibility is reasonably disputed, or legal verification is required.

We will make commercially reasonable efforts to process approved rewards within a reasonable period after receiving all required information.

We do not guarantee instant payment or payment through any particular provider.

Rewards are stated in U.S. dollars.

9. Taxes and Reporting

Referral rewards may constitute taxable income.

Referrers are responsible for their own applicable taxes and reporting obligations.

Crash Buddy Estimating may request a completed IRS Form W-9 or other appropriate tax documentation when legally required.

We may issue applicable tax reporting forms, including Form 1099, when required by law.

We may withhold or delay payment where necessary to comply with applicable tax reporting or withholding requirements.

Nothing in these Referral Terms constitutes tax advice.

10. Marketing, Privacy, and Consent

Referrers must respect the privacy of individuals and businesses they refer.

You must not submit personal information obtained unlawfully or without any required authorization.

You must comply with applicable laws governing email, text messages, advertising, privacy, and commercial communications.

Submitting a referral does not automatically subscribe the Referred Shop to marketing communications.

Crash Buddy Estimating may contact a Referred Shop as permitted by applicable law and our Privacy Policy.

Where a Referrer publicly recommends or promotes Crash Buddy Estimating and may receive compensation, the Referrer is responsible for making any legally required disclosure of that material connection.

Referrers must not represent themselves as employees, agents, or authorized spokespersons of Crash Buddy Estimating unless separately authorized.

11. Estimator Referrals

Estimators working with Crash Buddy Estimating may introduce body shops through approved referral methods.

An estimator referral does not grant:

  • Ownership of the Referred Shop relationship.
  • Exclusive rights to future estimating assignments.
  • Guaranteed future work.
  • Automatic increases in estimating compensation.
  • Recurring commissions.
  • Control over the Shop's account or Claims Hub workspace.

Management may separately consider a successful referral when reviewing an estimator's compensation.

Any compensation adjustment is discretionary unless expressly provided in a separate written agreement.

Any approved rate adjustment applies prospectively unless the written agreement states otherwise.

An estimator's eligibility for the standard US$250 reward may be subject to the estimator's separate contractor or compensation agreement.

12. No Employment or Agency Relationship

Participation in the Program does not create employment, partnership, franchise, joint venture, or agency relationships.

Referrers are independent participants and are not authorized to bind Crash Buddy LLC, make commitments on its behalf, or negotiate service terms for it.

Participation does not entitle a Referrer to future commissions or other compensation beyond rewards validly earned under the applicable Program terms.

13. Changes, Suspension, and Termination

Crash Buddy Estimating may modify, suspend, or discontinue the Program prospectively.

Changes will be communicated through updated Referral Terms or another reasonable method where appropriate.

Changes will not retroactively eliminate a reward already validly earned under the terms applicable when it was earned, except as required by law or where the reward was obtained through fraud or material misrepresentation.

We may suspend or terminate participation for fraud, abuse, unlawful conduct, or material violations of these Referral Terms.

Any existing referral that has not yet qualified when a Program change occurs will be handled under the applicable announced transition rules and legal requirements.

14. No Guarantee of Referrals or Revenue

Crash Buddy Estimating does not guarantee that any referred Shop will become a customer, place an order, complete three Supplements, or generate an eligible reward.

The Referrer is not entitled to payment merely for submitting a name, contact information, or referral form.

No recurring income or future business relationship is guaranteed.

15. Governing Law and General Terms

These Referral Terms are governed by California law, except where mandatory applicable law requires otherwise.

Any dispute concerning these Referral Terms is subject to applicable law and any controlling written agreement.

If a provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions will continue in effect to the extent permitted by law.

Failure to enforce a provision does not waive the right to enforce it later.

These Referral Terms govern the Program together with any separately applicable written referral agreement.

Where a separately signed agreement expressly governs referral compensation and conflicts with these Referral Terms, the signed agreement controls to the extent of the conflict.
