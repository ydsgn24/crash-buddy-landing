This Privacy Policy explains how Crash Buddy LLC, doing business as Crash Buddy Estimating (“Crash Buddy Estimating,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects personal information in connection with our website, remote collision estimating services, Claims Hub, order forms, referral program, estimator applications, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

Crash Buddy Estimating primarily serves collision repair businesses (“Shops”) and their authorized representatives. Our Services may involve information relating to a Shop's customers, vehicles, insurance claims, and repair documentation.

This Policy applies to information we process in operating our business and providing the Services. Certain claim-related information is provided to us by Shops, which are responsible for determining what information they are authorized to disclose.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our Services, we may collect the following categories of information.

Business and contact information: Names, business names, job titles, email addresses, telephone numbers, business addresses, and communications preferences.

Account information: Account identifiers, login credentials or authentication information, account roles, permissions, and related activity records.

Vehicle and claim information: Vehicle identification numbers (VINs), vehicle descriptions, damage photographs, insurance estimates, claim numbers, insurer information, repair documentation, and other information submitted in connection with an Estimate or Supplement.

Claims Hub content: Documents, photographs, messages, claim updates, estimating files, and other content provided by authorized users.

Order and transaction information: Services ordered, transaction dates, amounts, payment status, invoices, and related records. Payment card information may be collected and processed directly by our payment service providers. We do not intend to store complete payment card numbers in our own application systems.

Communications: Information submitted through contact forms, support requests, demonstration requests, email, and other communications.

Referral information: Information provided to identify a referring party, a referred Shop, and eligibility for referral benefits.

Estimator applications: Résumés, professional history, qualifications, credentials, portfolio information, and other information voluntarily submitted by applicants.

Technical information: IP addresses, browser and device information, approximate location derived from IP addresses, authentication logs, and information about interactions with the Services, to the extent collected by our systems or service providers.

Some uploaded documents may contain personal information about individuals who are not direct users of our Services.

Please provide only information reasonably necessary for the requested estimating work.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide Estimates, Supplements, Claims Hub, and related Services.
  • Receive, organize, review, and process estimating requests.
  • Create, administer, and secure authorized Shop accounts.
  • Communicate with Shops about orders, claims, documents, and service updates.
  • Facilitate communication between Shops and our estimating team.
  • Process payments and maintain transaction records.
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Schedule demonstrations and business meetings.
  • Administer referral programs.
  • Evaluate estimator applications and verify professional qualifications.
  • Maintain service reliability, investigate errors, and prevent unauthorized access, fraud, and abuse.
  • Improve the functionality, usability, and security of our Services.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our agreements.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We may also send promotional communications about our estimating services, Claims Hub, and related offerings where permitted by applicable law and subject to the choices described below.

Where applicable law requires consent or another specific legal basis for processing, we will comply with those requirements.

We do not authorize the use of Shop-submitted claim files for unrelated third-party advertising.

3. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to operate our business or provide the Services.

Recipients may include:

Service providers: Hosting providers, server and file storage providers, payment processors, email delivery services, communications platforms, security providers, technical support providers, and other vendors supporting our operations.

Estimating personnel: Authorized employees and independent contractors who need access to particular information to perform estimating services or related operational responsibilities.

Professional advisors: Attorneys, accountants, auditors, insurers, and other professional advisors when reasonably necessary.

Legal and regulatory recipients: Government authorities, courts, regulators, or other parties where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law.

Business transaction participants: Relevant parties in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable legal requirements.

We seek to limit access to information according to the recipient's role and legitimate business need.

Third-party providers may process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies, as well as any contractual obligations owed to us.

We do not disclose Shop claim files to unrelated third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

4. Shop Customer and Claim Information

Shops may submit information relating to vehicle owners, customers, insurers, repair claims, and other individuals.

Shops are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights, notices, permissions, and other lawful authority to collect and provide that information to Crash Buddy Estimating.

Shops should not upload information unrelated to the requested estimating services.

Crash Buddy Estimating uses claim-related information to perform the requested work, maintain related records, support Claims Hub operations, communicate with authorized Shop representatives, and fulfill other purposes described in this Policy that are consistent with applicable law and our agreements.

Where we process information on behalf of a Shop, we will address applicable requests and obligations in accordance with our contractual role and legal responsibilities.

We do not independently determine insurance coverage, approve claims, or make decisions on behalf of an insurance company.

5. Claims Hub Accounts and Access

Claims Hub is designed for authorized business users.

Account access may be managed through permissions associated with a Shop and its authorized personnel.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials and notifying us promptly if they suspect unauthorized account access.

Shops are responsible for identifying which individuals should have access to their Shop's workspace and informing us when access should be changed or revoked, subject to the available account-management procedures.

Authorized Crash Buddy personnel may access claim information when necessary to provide services, assist users, investigate technical issues, or maintain platform security.

Access permissions and security controls are intended to reduce unauthorized access but cannot eliminate every security risk.

6. Payment Processing

Payments may be handled through third-party payment processors.

These providers may collect payment information and process transactions under their own privacy policies and applicable payment-processing requirements.

Crash Buddy Estimating may receive limited transaction details, including payment status, amount, transaction identifiers, and billing information needed to administer orders and maintain business records.

Customers should review the applicable payment provider's privacy information when completing a transaction.

7. Marketing Communications

We may offer business customers and website visitors the opportunity to receive promotional emails about our estimating services, Claims Hub, service updates, and special offers.

We may use business contact information for marketing where permitted by applicable law.

Where consent is required, we will obtain it before sending marketing communications.

Marketing emails will include an appropriate unsubscribe mechanism.

You may also request to stop receiving promotional emails by contacting: crashbuddy.estimating@gmail.com

Opting out of promotional messages does not necessarily stop essential order confirmations, account notifications, security notices, invoices, or other service-related communications.

We may engage third-party email marketing providers in the future. If we do, their processing of information will be addressed through appropriate disclosures and applicable legal requirements.

Consent to marketing is not a condition of purchasing an Estimate or Supplement.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and Claims Hub may use cookies, local storage, session identifiers, and similar technologies to support functionality, authentication, security, preferences, and service performance.

Additional analytics or advertising technologies may be introduced in the future.

If implemented, such technologies may collect information about website interactions, subject to applicable notices, consent requirements, and user choices.

Where required by law, we will provide appropriate notices and mechanisms for managing nonessential technologies.

Users may manage certain cookies and browser storage settings through their browsers. Disabling essential technologies may affect the functionality of the Services.

9. Server Storage of Claim Files

Vehicle photographs, claim documents, Estimates, Supplements, and related files are stored on servers supporting our Services.

These files may be accessed by authorized personnel as reasonably necessary to prepare estimating documents, communicate with Shops, provide support, maintain Claims Hub, and perform related operational functions.

We seek to limit access according to legitimate business needs.

We do not guarantee permanent storage, uninterrupted availability, or that every uploaded file can be recovered after deletion or a technical failure.

Shops should retain their own copies of important claim and repair documentation.

10. Security and Confidentiality

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss.

Our safeguards are intended to reflect the nature of the information and the risks associated with its processing.

Estimating work may be performed by authorized team members, including independent contractors.

We seek to apply appropriate confidentiality obligations and access restrictions to personnel who handle customer information.

However, no website, electronic transmission, storage system, or security measure can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We cannot guarantee that unauthorized access or other security incidents will never occur.

If a security incident occurs, we will evaluate and address applicable notification and response obligations under relevant law.

11. Data Minimization and Sensitive Information

We ask Shops and other users to provide only the information reasonably necessary to complete the requested Services.

Please avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive information, including Social Security numbers, financial account credentials, medical records, or other sensitive personal details unrelated to the estimating request.

Where practical, redact unrelated personal information from documents before uploading them.

If sensitive information is included in a submitted claim file, we will handle it according to our applicable obligations and the purposes for which it was provided.

12. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services and fulfill legitimate business and legal purposes.

Retention periods may depend on the type of information, the nature of the customer relationship, contractual obligations, legal requirements, security considerations, and the need to maintain transaction or claim-related records.

We may retain certain records after an order is completed or an account becomes inactive where reasonably necessary for accounting, compliance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or enforcement of agreements.

When information is no longer required, we take appropriate steps to delete, de-identify, or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and technical limitations.

Claims Hub should not be treated as a guaranteed permanent archive or the Shop's sole recordkeeping system.

Shops should maintain their own copies of important claim and repair documentation.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, the nature of the information, and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to certain personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible information.
  • Request a portable copy of certain information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable.
  • Opt out of certain data sales, sharing, or targeted advertising where applicable.
  • Exercise applicable privacy rights without unlawful discrimination or retaliation.

Not all rights apply in every jurisdiction or to every category of information.

To submit a privacy request, email: crashbuddy.estimating@gmail.com

Please include enough information for us to understand and evaluate your request.

We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity or authority before responding.

If your request concerns information submitted by a Shop about its customer, we may need to coordinate with the Shop or direct you to the Shop, depending on our role and applicable law.

We will respond within the periods required by applicable law.

14. State Privacy Notices

Residents of certain U.S. states, including California, may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

Whether a particular law applies to Crash Buddy LLC depends on the relevant statutory requirements, including business activities and applicability thresholds.

Where a state privacy law applies, we will provide the notices, request mechanisms, and other protections required by that law.

Any required disclosures concerning categories of information, retention, sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or sensitive information must reflect our actual processing practices.

This Policy should not be interpreted as a representation that every state privacy statute applies to our business.

15. Third-Party Services and Video Meetings

Our Services may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party platforms for payments, meetings, communications, or other functions.

If you choose to participate in a demonstration or meeting through a third-party video platform, that provider may collect information such as your name, email address, device information, and meeting participation details.

Third-party services may have their own privacy practices.

Crash Buddy Estimating does not control the independent privacy practices of third parties.

16. Estimator and Careers Applications

If you apply to join our estimating network or submit a Careers application, we may use your information to:

  • Review your application.
  • Evaluate professional qualifications and experience.
  • Verify credentials where appropriate.
  • Communicate about potential opportunities.
  • Maintain application and recruiting records as permitted by law.

Submission of an application does not guarantee an interview, acceptance, employment, contractor engagement, or work assignments.

We may retain application information for legitimate recruiting, recordkeeping, and legal purposes, subject to applicable law.

17. International Processing

Our Services may involve users, personnel, or service providers located in different jurisdictions, including the United States and Canada.

Information may be processed or stored in locations outside the jurisdiction where it was originally collected.

Where cross-border transfers are subject to applicable legal requirements, we will take steps required by those laws.

18. Children's Privacy

Our Services are intended for businesses, authorized business representatives, and adult applicants.

They are not directed to children under 18.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information directly from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact: crashbuddy.estimating@gmail.com

We will review the situation and take appropriate action.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our Services, business practices, technology, or legal obligations.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date.

Where required or appropriate, we may provide additional notice of material changes through the website, Claims Hub, email, or another reasonable method.

The version published on our website will govern from its stated effective date, subject to applicable law.
