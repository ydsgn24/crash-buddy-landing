Common questions

How do I get started?

Send one job now: request an Estimate ($105) or a Supplement ($145) above. No account needed. For ongoing work, request a demo and we set up Claims Hub for your Shop.

Can I send just one job?

Yes. No account needed. The finished PDF comes to your email.

What do you need to write a Supplement?

The current insurance Estimate (PDF or CCC export), teardown photos — wide shots and close-ups — and any denial notes. A VIN helps. For an initial Estimate, photos and vehicle details are enough. Anything else, your estimator asks for inside the claim.

How fast is turnaround?

Most Supplements come back in about 6 hours and most Estimates in about 2 hours, during business hours (Pacific), once we have clear photos and — for a Supplement — the current insurance Estimate. Heavy structural files can take longer; we tell you when we open the file.

Do you write in Mitchell or Audatex?

We write in CCC ONE and deliver a PDF, so there is no extra software seat for the work you send us. If your Shop works in Mitchell or Audatex, ask before you order and we’ll tell you what we can do.

Do you talk to the insurer?

No. Your Shop reviews, approves and submits under its own name and rates, and handles the adjuster. We write the documentation your Shop submits.

How do I pay?

Per job, when you order: $105 per Estimate, $145 per Supplement, no subscription or minimum. Pricing agreed for your Shop appears in Claims Hub before you pay. Specialty vehicles and research-intensive jobs are quoted before you order.

How does Claims Hub work?

One claim per vehicle. Photos, documents, messages with your estimator and every Estimate or Supplement stay on it, so the next Supplement starts from the same claim — not from scratch. Your Shop’s rates and agreed pricing live there too, and archived claims can be reopened.

Do you work with Canadian shops?

Yes — remotely, across the U.S. and Canada, through Claims Hub.

Who can see my claims?

Your Shop and the Crash Buddy estimators working on your jobs. Each Shop has its own private workspace. See our Privacy Policy.
