What you’ll actually learn

Seven practical skill areas, taught the way the work is done every week.

  1. Build an Estimate

    Turn damage photos, vehicle information and claim documentation into an initial repair Estimate.

  2. Write Supplements

    Document additional damage and build Supplements from existing Estimates and teardown findings.

  3. Read insurer Estimates

    Understand what is included, what may be missing, and what needs closer review.

  4. Repair vs. replace

    Learn how estimators approach common repair-versus-replace decisions.

  5. Labor & operations

    Understand labor operations, common line items, rates and the estimating logic behind them.

  6. Document the file

    Use photos, notes, documents and supporting information to build a clear claim file.

  7. Review your work

    Learn how to review an Estimate or Supplement before it leaves your desk.

Common questions

Who is this training for?

Shop employees moving toward estimating, body shop owners who want to train someone internally, and people entering collision estimating.

Do I need collision estimating experience?

No. Shop or collision repair experience helps, but the training starts from the actual workflow, not from prior estimating experience.

What will I learn?

How Estimates and Supplements are actually built: reading the claim and the insurer’s Estimate, repair vs. replace decisions, labor and operations, documenting the file, and reviewing your own work.

Is this a certification program?

No. It is practical skills training from working estimators; it does not include a certification or job placement.
